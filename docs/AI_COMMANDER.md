# ResQLink AI Commander Decision Intelligence

## 1. Overview
The **AI Commander** acts as a co-pilot for Incident Commanders and Field Leaders, delivering real-time decision recommendations, resource optimization, bottleneck avoidance, and situation synthesis.

---

## 2. Capabilities & Sub-Screens
1. **AI Chat & Natural Language Querying (`/rescue/ai/chat`)**:
   - Interactive tactical dialogue regarding hazard progression, shelter capacities, and logistics rerouting.
2. **Incident Predictive Analysis (`/rescue/ai/analysis`)**:
   - Machine learning inference evaluating flood breach rates, casualty vulnerability, and critical structural failure windows.
3. **Actionable Recommendations (`/rescue/ai/recommendations`)**:
   - Ranked tactical suggestions with confidence scores (e.g., pre-staging amphibious boats, rerouting medical convoys).
   - One-tap "Authorize Dispatch" triggers.
4. **Automated Situation Summaries (`/rescue/ai/summary`)**:
   - Real-time generation of executive SitReps summarizing operations across 13 districts.
